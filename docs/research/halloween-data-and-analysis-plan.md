# Research data and analysis methodology

This document retains implementation knowledge, not conclusions from a previous investigation.

Use a fresh operator-defined niche. Etsy listing observations include listing/shop IDs, title/description/tags, listing type, original creation timestamp, views/favorites and currency-qualified price. Unknown fields stay unknown. Keep immutable sanitized page evidence with query, offset, observation time and digest.

Code normalizes types and currency, filters physical records, deduplicates IDs and measures coverage. Models refine semantic intent and interpret large cohorts. Review self-critique and source-reference checks before passing recommendations onward. Never infer competitor verified monthly listing revenue from views, favorites or shop lifetime transactions.

Sample public shop context across engagement quantiles rather than only early-ranked shops. Bound shop and listing review probes, retain timestamps and negative/empty observations, and remove reviewer identifiers. Review frequency is a purchase proxy, not a calibrated sales conversion model. Ranked convenience samples are not representative market estimates.

Business reasoning optimizes expected recurring receipts relative to total operating cost, especially manual effort. Cheap experiments need not prove demand first. Specific product hypotheses must expose automation, effort uncertainty, counterevidence and season-aligned experiment rules. Actual published-product performance remains the eventual feedback source.

Do not import historical business conclusions into a fresh investigation. Normal incremental analysis can reuse conclusions only within the active investigation's retained evidence/catalog. Explicit research clearing removes those priors, not operational spending history.
